01The 30-second pitch

For a digital marketing or web agency. Say it in this order.

Who
Digital marketing and web agencies, and freelancers, whose clients keep asking for an AI chatbot.
Problem
Clients want an assistant that answers from their own documents, and you cannot risk one that makes things up. Website projects end, and the income ends with them.
What it does
cob-rag-engine is a finished platform you run on your own server: a separate private assistant for every client, a website widget with leads and a WhatsApp hand-off button, English, Hindi and Hinglish, plans and usage counted per client. Your name, logo and colours on the dashboard and e-mails.
Proof
412 of 412 isolation attacks refused in the repository's own test. Every price, date and phone number in an answer must appear in the client's documents, or the assistant says it does not know. Search measured on six public datasets (mean nDCG@10 0.537 against 0.526 published for the same embedding model). All numbers are in the benchmark report.
Ask
Try it free with one client. Write to callobuzz@gmail.com, and install it yourself or ask us to help set it up.

How you earn. Charge each client a monthly fee for their assistant. You set the price, in ₹ or $. Plans, caps and usage counted per client keep the bill fair. Your costs are the server and the AI calls (a client can bring their own AI key). What you earn depends on your price and your clients; no income is promised here.

02How it works

You run one engine. Each client gets their own private assistant on it.

  1. Install it, or ask for help. Docker on your own server; the guide is step by step.
  2. Add a client's documents. PDF, Word, Excel, web pages and scans, including Hindi scans.
  3. Put it on their website. One script tag, locked to their domains, with a WhatsApp button for hand-off to a person.
  4. Bill them every month. Usage is counted per client, so the invoice is easy to explain.

03Why not LangChain, LlamaIndex, Dify or an agent SDK?

Say it in four lines.

  1. LangChain, LlamaIndex and the agent SDKs give you parts to build with; cob-rag-engine is the finished knowledge platform: customers, keys, documents, fact-checked answers, a website widget and billing, already built and tested.
  2. They are not rivals: your LangChain, LlamaIndex, OpenAI or Claude agent plugs into it over MCP and uses it as its governed knowledge base.
  3. Dify is a full platform too, but it is built for designing apps, and its licence needs written authorization to run it for many customers; cob-rag-engine is built to resell, with plans and billing, Hindi and Hinglish, WhatsApp and DPDP consent, and answers that stay inside the customer's own documents.
  4. So you sell assistants from day one instead of building the platform under them, and if all you need is a one-off prototype, LangChain alone is the right call.

The longer comparison is in the README.
