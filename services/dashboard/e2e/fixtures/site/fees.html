Fees for 2026-27

All fees are for the full academic year, from June to March, and include study material and weekly tests.

  • Class 8 and Class 9 (SSC or CBSE): ₹32,000 a year.
  • Class 10 (SSC or CBSE), with board exam practice papers: ₹38,000 a year.
  • Class 11 and Class 12 science (PCM): ₹55,000 a year.
  • JEE or MHT-CET foundation, added to Class 11 or 12: ₹25,000 a year.

You can pay in three instalments: 40 percent at admission, 30 percent in October and 30 percent in January. We accept UPI, bank transfer and cheques. A sibling discount of 10 percent applies to the second child. There are no hidden charges; the registration fee of ₹1,000 is adjusted against the first instalment.
