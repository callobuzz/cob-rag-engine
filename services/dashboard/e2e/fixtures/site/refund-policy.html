Refund policy

If you withdraw within 15 days of the batch start date, we refund the full fee paid except the registration fee of ₹1,000. After 15 days and before 31 August, we refund 50 percent of the instalments paid. After 31 August no refund is possible, but the balance can be moved to a sibling's admission in the same year.

Refunds are sent to the same UPI ID or bank account within 10 working days of a written request at the front desk or by e-mail to admissions@vidyaclasses.in. Study material already handed over is not refunded.
