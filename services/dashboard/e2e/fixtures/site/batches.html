Batches and timings

Weekday batches run Monday to Friday. Class 8 and 9 meet from 4 pm to 5:30 pm, Class 10 from 5:30 pm to 7:30 pm, and Class 11 and 12 science from 7 am to 9 am before college. Weekend batches for Class 11 and 12 run on Saturday and Sunday from 9 am to 1 pm.

Each batch has at most 25 students. Doubt-solving sessions are held every Saturday from 3 pm to 5 pm and are free for enrolled students. Parents receive a monthly progress report on WhatsApp, and a parent-teacher meeting is held once a term at the Kothrud centre.
