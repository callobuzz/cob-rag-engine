Vidya Classes, Kothrud

Vidya Classes has coached students in Kothrud, Pune since 2011. We teach Mathematics, Science and English for Classes 8 to 10 (SSC and CBSE) and Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for Classes 11 and 12, with separate JEE and MHT-CET foundation batches. Every batch has at most 25 students so that each child is seen and heard.
