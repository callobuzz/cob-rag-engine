# Commercial licence

cob-rag-engine is open source. You can use it for free under the terms below.

## What is open

- The engine is under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The full text is in `LICENSE`.
- The client SDKs (`clients/js` and `clients/python`) are under the MIT licence.
- The widget scripts that run on your website are under the MIT licence.

A business that adds the widget to its website, or calls the SDK from its own app, keeps its own code under
its own terms.

## When you need a commercial licence

You may need a commercial licence in two cases:

- You change the engine and let people use it over a network, but you do not want to offer them the source code of your changed version
  under AGPL-3.0 (AGPL section 13).
- Your company policy does not allow AGPL software.

If you run the engine without changes, or you offer your changed source code to its users under AGPL-3.0, you do not need one.
