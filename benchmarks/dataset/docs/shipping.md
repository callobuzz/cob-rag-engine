# Shipping and Delivery — Summit Trail Outfitters

## Delivery options within the United States
- Standard ground: 4–7 business days, free on orders over $75, otherwise $7.50.
- Expedited: 2–3 business days, $14.
- Overnight: next business day if ordered before 1 p.m. Pacific time, $29.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and Japan. International
orders take 7–14 business days. Duties and import taxes are collected at checkout for the UK,
EU and Australia, so there are no surprise charges on delivery. For Canada and Japan the
carrier collects duties from the recipient.

## Items we cannot ship
Fuel canisters (butane, isobutane, white gas) and bear spray ship only by ground within the
contiguous United States. They cannot ship to Alaska, Hawaii, PO boxes, or internationally.

## Tracking
Every order gets a tracking link by email once it leaves the warehouse. Tracking numbers for
ground parcels start with 1Z; expedited and overnight use a 12-digit number.

## Lost or damaged parcels
Report a parcel that arrives damaged within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo of the box and the
item. We ship a replacement within 2 business days at no charge. Parcels marked delivered but not
received must be reported within 10 days so we can open a carrier claim.
