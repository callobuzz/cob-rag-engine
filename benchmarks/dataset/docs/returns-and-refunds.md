# Returns and Refunds Policy — Summit Trail Outfitters

## Standard return window
Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full
refund to the original payment method. Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the
returned parcel is scanned at our Reno fulfilment centre.

## Opened and used items
Opened items that show no signs of use may be returned within 30 days for store credit only.
Footwear that has been worn outdoors cannot be returned, except under the warranty policy.
Opened electronics (headlamps, GPS units, satellite messengers) carry a 15 percent restocking fee.

## Final-sale items
Clearance items marked "Final Sale", gift cards, and custom-embroidered apparel cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
Log in to your account, open the order (order numbers start with STO- followed by eight digits,
for example STO-40218877), and choose "Start a return". A prepaid label is emailed within one hour.
Return shipping is free for members of the Basecamp loyalty program; everyone else pays a flat
$6.95 label fee, deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges
Size exchanges are free and ship as soon as the original item is scanned. If the replacement size
is out of stock we refund the item instead.
