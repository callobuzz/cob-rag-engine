# Warranty — Summit Trail Outfitters

## Lifetime guarantee on house-brand gear
Products sold under our own Summit Trail label (tents, packs, sleeping bags and outerwear) carry
a lifetime guarantee against defects in materials and workmanship. Normal wear and tear, damage
from accidents, and damage from improper care are not covered.

## Third-party brands
Items from other manufacturers follow the manufacturer's warranty. We will help you file the
claim, but the manufacturer decides the outcome.

## Footwear
Summit Trail boots are covered for 2 years against sole separation and seam failure, even when
they have been worn outdoors.

## Electronics
Headlamps, GPS units and satellite messengers have a 1-year limited warranty. Batteries are
covered for 6 months.

## Making a warranty claim
Email warranty@summittrail.example with your order number, photos of the defect, and a short
description. Claims are reviewed within 7 business days. Approved claims receive a repair, a
replacement, or store credit — our choice. Keep your receipt: without proof of purchase we can
only offer repair at cost.

## Repair service
Our Boulder repair shop fixes zippers, seams and tent poles for any brand. Zipper replacement
costs $25, tent-pole sections $12 each. Turnaround is about 3 weeks.
