# Product Care Guide — Summit Trail Outfitters

## Down sleeping bags and jackets
Wash down gear in a front-loading machine on a gentle cycle with a down-specific soap. Tumble dry
on low heat with three clean tennis balls to break up clumps; a full dry takes 3 to 4 hours.
Never dry-clean down. Store sleeping bags loosely in the large cotton storage sack, not in the
compression stuff sack.

## Waterproof shells
Wash waterproof jackets with a technical cleaner, then restore the durable water repellent (DWR)
finish by tumble drying on medium heat for 20 minutes. If water no longer beads, apply a
spray-on DWR treatment.

## Tents
Always dry a tent completely before storing it to prevent mildew. Clean with cold water and a
sponge only — never machine wash a tent. Seam sealer should be reapplied every 2 years of
regular use.

## Leather boots
Brush off dirt after every hike, let boots dry at room temperature away from direct heat, and
apply a leather conditioner every 3 months of regular use.

## Product codes
Product codes (SKUs) use the format ST- plus a category letter and four digits. Sleeping bags use
the letter B (for example ST-B2041, the Alpine 20 bag), tents use T (ST-T1180, the Ridgeline 2
tent), and jackets use J (ST-J3302, the Storm Shell).
