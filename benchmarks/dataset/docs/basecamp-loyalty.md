# Basecamp Loyalty Program — Summit Trail Outfitters

## Joining
Basecamp membership is free. Sign up online or in any store with an email address.

## Earning points
Members earn 1 point per dollar spent, and 2 points per dollar on house-brand Summit Trail gear.
Points post to your account 14 days after an order ships, once the return window for most items
has started running.

## Tiers
- Trailhead: 0–499 points in a calendar year.
- Ridge: 500–1,499 points — free expedited shipping on every order.
- Summit: 1,500 points or more — free expedited shipping, early access to sales, and one free
  repair per year at the Boulder repair shop.

## Rewards
Every 200 points converts automatically into a $10 reward certificate. Certificates expire 90
days after they are issued and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Member benefits
All members get free return shipping labels and a birthday coupon worth 15 percent off one item.

## Points expiry
Points expire if an account has no purchase for 24 months.
