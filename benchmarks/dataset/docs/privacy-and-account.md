# Privacy and Your Account — Summit Trail Outfitters

## What we collect
We collect your name, contact details, order history and, if you join Basecamp, your points
balance. Payment card numbers are handled by our payment processor and are never stored on our
servers.

## Marketing email
You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time with the link at the bottom of every
message. Order and shipping emails are transactional and are always sent.

## Deleting your account
Request account deletion from the Privacy page in your account settings. We delete personal data
within 30 days, except records we must keep for 7 years for tax purposes. Unused Basecamp points
and reward certificates are forfeited when an account is deleted.

## Data requests
You can download a copy of your data from the same Privacy page; the export is emailed within
72 hours.

## Password and security
Passwords must be at least 12 characters. After 5 failed sign-in attempts an account is locked
for 15 minutes. Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app is available and recommended.
