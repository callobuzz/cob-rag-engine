# Size and Fit Guide — Summit Trail Outfitters

## Jackets and base layers
Our jackets are cut for layering: if you are between sizes, choose your usual size for a shell
and size down for a fitted base layer. Chest measurements: Small 36–38 inches, Medium 39–41,
Large 42–44, X-Large 45–47.

## Boots
Summit Trail boots run half a size small. Order half a size up from your usual street-shoe size,
and try them on in the afternoon, when feet are at their largest.

## Packs
Pack sizes are based on torso length, not height. Measure from the bony bump at the base of your
neck (the C7 vertebra) to the top of your hip bones. Small fits 15–17 inch torsos, Medium 17–19,
Large 19–21.

## Sleeping bags
Choose a bag at least 6 inches longer than your height. The Regular length fits people up to
6 feet; the Long length fits people up to 6 feet 6 inches.

## Kids' sizes
Kids' jackets are sized by age from 4 to 14 and include a grow-with-me cuff extension that adds
about one year of wear.
