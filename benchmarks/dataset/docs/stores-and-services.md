# Stores and Services — Summit Trail Outfitters

## Locations
We have four stores: Boulder, Colorado (the flagship, with the repair shop); Reno, Nevada (next to
the fulfilment centre); Bend, Oregon; and Missoula, Montana.

## Opening hours
Stores are open Monday to Saturday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Sunday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. All stores close
on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

## Rentals
The Boulder and Bend stores rent tents, packs and snowshoes. A 2-person tent rents for $25 per
day; snowshoes rent for $15 per day. A refundable $100 deposit is required for each rental.

## Buy online, pick up in store
Orders placed online before noon can be picked up the same day at any store. Orders are held for
7 days before they are cancelled and refunded.

## Price match
We match the advertised price of an identical item from a major US retailer at the time of
purchase, and for 30 days after purchase if the price drops on our own site.

## Workshops
Free weekend workshops cover map-and-compass navigation, stove maintenance and Leave No Trace
basics. Registration opens 2 weeks before each workshop.
