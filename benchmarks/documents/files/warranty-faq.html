Warranty questions

How long is the warranty on tents?

Tents carry a 7-year warranty against manufacturing defects in the fabric, poles and zips. Damage from fire, UV exposure or pets is not covered.

Are sleeping bags covered?

Sleeping bags are covered for 3 years. Loss of loft from compressing a bag for long storage is normal wear and is not a defect.

What about boots?

Boots have a 12-month warranty on the sole bond and stitching. Resoling by our partner workshop costs 65 dollars and restarts a 6-month workmanship guarantee.

How do I make a claim?

Bring the item and proof of purchase to any store, or start online with claim form W-3. Claims are assessed within 10 business days.

Do you repair gear from other brands?

Yes. Our repair desk fixes zips, seams and punctures on any brand, from 20 dollars.
