Trekking boots take the most abuse of anything we sell, so their warranty is written around the sole bond and the stitching.

Boot warranty

What is covered on boots?

Boots have a 12-month warranty on the sole bond and stitching. Worn lugs, scuffed leather and laces are normal wear and are not defects. Waterproof membranes are covered for 18 months if the boots were proofed with our own wax.

Can my boots be resoled?

Yes. Our partner workshop in Dehradun resoles boots in 10 working days and restarts a 6-month workmanship guarantee on the new sole.
