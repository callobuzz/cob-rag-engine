Tent warranty

What is covered on a tent?

Tents carry a 7-year warranty on the fabric, poles and zips against manufacturing defects. Damage from fire, UV exposure left by long storage in a car, or pets is not covered. The rain fly seam tape is covered for 2 years.

How do I claim?

Bring the tent and the invoice to any store, or fill claim form T-12 online. A tent claim is assessed within 7 business days and a pole section is posted free to any pin code in India.
