A sleeping bag is only as warm as its loft, which is what this warranty protects.

Sleeping bag warranty

What is covered on sleeping bags?

Sleeping bags are covered for 3 years against baffle failures, broken zips and down leaking through the shell. Loss of loft from compressing a bag in its stuff sack for months is normal wear and is not a defect.

What if my bag has lost its loft?

Send it to our Bengaluru service desk. A bag that is washed and re-filled with the original down gets a fresh 12-month guarantee on the new fill.
