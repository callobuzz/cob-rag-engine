# Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to everything you buy from **{{legal_name}}** on **{{product_name}}**: plan fees,
balance top-ups and add-ons. Effective {{effective_date}}.

## Cancelling

- You can cancel your plan at any time from your workspace.
- Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. You keep full access until
  then, and you are not charged again.
- We do not refund a month that has already started.

## Unused balance

If you close your workspace or no longer want to use {{product_name}}, ask us at {{support_email}} and we
will refund your unused prepaid balance. We send the refund within 7 working days of your request, to
the same payment method you used.

## Failed or duplicate payments

If money left your account but the payment did not complete, or you were charged twice, the amount is
refunded automatically to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. You do not need to
ask. If it has not arrived after 7 working days, write to us with the payment reference.

## Add-ons and plan fees

An add-on or plan fee that has started to be used is not refundable. If something we sold you did not
work as described, write to us within 7 days and we will either fix it or refund it.

## How to ask

E-mail {{support_email}} from the address on your account. Include your workspace name and the payment
reference, and tell us what you want refunded. We reply within 2 working days.

## Taxes

A refund includes the GST that was charged on the refunded amount. We issue a credit note for it.
