# Data Processing Addendum

This addendum is part of the Terms of Service between **{{legal_name}}** ("processor", "we") and the
business using **{{product_name}}** ("business", "data fiduciary"). It applies to the personal data of
the business's visitors and customers that we process on the business's behalf. Effective
{{effective_date}}.

## 1. Roles

The business decides why and how its visitors' personal data is processed and is the data fiduciary
under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We are the data processor: we process that data
only to provide the service.

## 2. Instructions

We process personal data only on the business's documented instructions, which are these terms, the
settings the business chooses in its workspace and what it asks us in writing. If an instruction would
break the law, we will tell the business.

## 3. The business's duties

The business is responsible for having a lawful basis and giving its visitors the notice and obtaining
the consent the law requires. The chat widget shows a notice and, where a visitor leaves contact
details, asks for consent; the business must keep the wording accurate for its own use.

## 4. Confidentiality and security

People who work with this data are bound by confidentiality. We use encryption in transit, encryption
of sensitive stored fields, access controls, audit logs and regular backups, and we keep these measures
up to date.

## 5. Sub-processors

The business agrees that we may use the sub-processors listed on our Sub-processors page. We tell the
business before we add or replace one, and the business may object within 30 days by writing to
{{support_email}}. We stay responsible for our sub-processors.

## 6. Breaches

We tell the business without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware, of a personal data
breach affecting its data, with what we know about what happened and what we are doing about it.

## 7. Helping with requests

We help the business answer requests from visitors to see, correct or erase their data, and help with
any complaint or inquiry from the Data Protection Board of India or another authority.

## 8. Deleting data

When the agreement ends, or when the business asks, we delete the business's visitors' data within 30
days. We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices, and delete that when the legal
period ends. Backups are removed as they expire.

## 9. Proof and audit

We answer the business's reasonable security and privacy questionnaires once a year and give the
information needed to show we follow this addendum. A site audit is not included, unless a regulator
requires one.

## 10. Where data is processed

The Sub-processors page lists each sub-processor and the country where it processes data. We tell the
business before data starts to go to a new country.

## Annex A: what is processed

- **Subjects:** the business's website visitors and customers who use the assistant.
- **Data:** their questions, the assistant's answers, and contact details they choose to give.
- **Purpose:** answering the visitor, showing the business the conversation and passing on leads.
- **Duration:** as long as the business keeps conversations, and then as section 8 says.
