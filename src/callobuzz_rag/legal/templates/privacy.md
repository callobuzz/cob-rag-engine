# Privacy Notice

This notice explains how **{{legal_name}}** ("we"), operating **{{product_name}}**, handles personal
data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, and the Information Technology
Act, 2000 with its rules. Effective {{effective_date}}.

## 1. Two roles

- **Your account and billing: we are the data fiduciary.** When you (or a member of your team) create a
  workspace, sign in, pay or contact support, we decide why and how that data is used.
- **Your customers' conversations: we are a data processor.** When a visitor talks to a business's
  assistant, that business is the data fiduciary. We process the visitor's messages only on the
  business's instructions, as set out in our Data Processing Addendum. The business's own privacy
  notice is shown to its visitors in the chat.

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | Why we use it |
|---|---|
| Name, e-mail, password (stored as a one-way hash) | To create and secure your account and sign you in |
| Business name, address, GSTIN | To bill you and issue tax invoices |
| Payment status and references from our payment partner (we never see your full card number) | To record payments, top-ups and refunds |
| Content you add: documents, website pages, answers | To run your assistant |
| Visitors' questions and the assistant's answers, and contact details a visitor chooses to leave | To answer them, show you the conversation and, if the visitor agrees, pass on a lead |
| Technical logs: IP address, browser, time | Security, abuse prevention and fixing faults |

We use data only for these purposes and only with the consent or other lawful ground the Act allows,
such as performing our contract with you or meeting a legal duty.

## 3. Who else sees data

- **AI model providers.** To produce an answer, a visitor's question and the matching passages of your
  content are sent to an AI model provider. We mask phone numbers and e-mail addresses in the question
  before it is sent.
- **Hosting, e-mail and payment providers** that run parts of the service for us. They are listed on our
  Sub-processors page.
- Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Account data: while your workspace exists, and for a short period after you close it.
- Tax and billing records: 8 years, because the law requires us to keep them.
- Conversations: for the period the business sets, and then deleted.
- Technical logs: up to 1 year.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, erase it, or nominate someone to act
for you if you cannot. Write to {{grievance_email}} or {{support_email}}. We answer within 90 days, and
usually much sooner. For a visitor's chat, please also ask the business you spoke to.

## 6. Security and breaches

We protect data with encryption in transit, encrypted storage of sensitive fields, access controls and
audit logs. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of
India as the law requires.

## 7. Children

The service is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child's
data reached us, tell us and we will remove it.

## 8. Cookies

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We
do not use advertising cookies.

First write to our Grievance Officer, {{grievance_name}}, {{grievance_email}}, {{grievance_address}}. If
you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
