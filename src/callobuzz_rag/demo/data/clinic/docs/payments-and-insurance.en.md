# Payments and insurance

## How you can pay

- UPI (any app: PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and others). The QR code is at the reception desk.
- Debit and credit cards.
- Cash.

We do not take cheques. The receipt is given after payment. A GST invoice is available on request.

## Insurance

The clinic works with three insurance desks for cashless treatment. You will need your TPA card or e-card and a photo ID.

| Insurer desk | How it works |
|---|---|
| Suraksha Health desk | Cashless through your TPA card |
| Bharat Care desk | Cashless through your TPA card |
| Aarogya Plus desk | Cashless through your TPA card |

For any other insurance, please pay at the clinic. We give you a receipt and a bill with the doctor's registration number so that you can claim it back from your insurer. Cashless approval can take up to one working day, so please tell the reception before your visit.

## Refunds

A consultation fee is not refunded once the doctor has seen you. If the clinic cancels your slot, the fee is returned the same day by UPI or in cash.

## Questions about a bill

Please speak to the reception at the clinic or call the reception on +91 98765 43210. We check any bill within one working day.
