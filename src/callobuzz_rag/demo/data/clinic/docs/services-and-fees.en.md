# Services and fees

All fees are in rupees and include GST where it applies. The reception can give a printed fee list on request.

## Consultation

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| First consultation (adult or child) | ₹500 |
| Follow-up within 7 days of the first visit | ₹300 |
| Follow-up after 7 days | ₹500 |
| Certificate (fitness, school, leave) | ₹150 |

## Tests and procedures at the clinic

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| ECG | ₹400 |
| Blood sugar test (finger prick) | ₹80 |
| Nebulisation | ₹200 |
| Dressing of a small wound | ₹250 |

Blood tests that need a laboratory are collected at the clinic and sent to a partner laboratory. The laboratory sends its own price list, and reports reach the clinic the next working day.

## Vaccinations for children

Dr. Rohan Deshpande follows the national child vaccination schedule. The vaccine is charged at its printed price, and there is no extra injection fee. Please bring the child's vaccination card to every visit.

## Home visits

Dr. Anjali Kulkarni makes home visits for elderly or bed-ridden patients within 5 km of the clinic, which covers most of Kothrud and Karve Nagar. A home visit costs ₹1,200. To ask for one, call the reception on +91 98765 43210. Home visits are not available on Sundays and are not for emergencies.

## What we do not do

We do not give medical advice on chat, over e-mail or by WhatsApp. We do not provide X-ray, MRI or surgery at the clinic. We will refer you to a hospital when you need these.
