# Booking an appointment

## How to book

1. Call the reception between 09:00 and 13:00 or between 17:00 and 21:00, Monday to Saturday. The number is +91 98765 43210.
2. Or send a WhatsApp message to the same number. The reception replies during clinic hours.
3. Tell the reception the patient's name, age, which doctor you want to see and your preferred day and time.

The reception confirms the slot by a call or a message. Online payment is not needed to book.

## Walk-ins

Walk-in patients are welcome. Booked patients are seen first, so a walk-in may wait about 30 to 45 minutes in the evening.

## Children

Dr. Rohan Deshpande sees children. Evening slots between 17:00 and 20:00 fill up quickly, so please book a day ahead. For a tomorrow-evening visit, call the reception in the morning to check if a slot is free.

## Changing or cancelling

Please call the reception at least 2 hours before your slot. There is no charge for cancelling.

## What to bring

- Any earlier prescriptions and test reports.
- The child's vaccination card, for a child.
- A photo ID if you want a certificate.

## Waiting time and queue

Each visit takes about 15 minutes. If the doctor is delayed by an emergency, the reception will let you know.
