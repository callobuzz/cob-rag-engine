# About Nirmal Family Clinic

Nirmal Family Clinic is a family clinic on Karve Road in Kothrud, Pune. It has served families in Kothrud, Karve Nagar, Erandwane and Bavdhan for more than twelve years. This is a fictional clinic used for a product demo.

## Our doctors

- **Dr. Anjali Kulkarni** is the general physician. She sees adults and older patients for fever, infections, blood pressure, diabetes follow-ups and general check-ups.
- **Dr. Rohan Deshpande** is the children's doctor. He sees babies and children for vaccinations, growth checks, fever, cough and stomach problems.

## Where we are

Plot 12, Karve Road, Kothrud, Pune 411038. The clinic is on the ground floor, next to the pharmacy. There is a ramp at the entrance, and two-wheeler parking in front of the building.

## Clinic timings

- Monday to Saturday: 09:00 to 13:00 and 17:00 to 21:00.
- Sunday: 10:00 to 13:00 only. The clinic is closed on Sunday evening.
- The clinic is closed on public holidays. The reception puts a notice at the door and answers calls on the morning of the holiday.

## Contact

Call or send a WhatsApp message to the reception on +91 98765 43210. The front desk e-mail is frontdesk@nirmalclinic.example.com for non-urgent questions. The reception answers e-mail within one working day.

## In an emergency

The clinic is not an emergency room. For chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, fainting or a serious accident, call 108 or go to the nearest hospital at once. Do not wait for an appointment.
