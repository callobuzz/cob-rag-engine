Your assistant is built so a visitor is never stuck, never misled about talking to an AI, and an owner is never surprised. This page collects what it promises. Each part below is switched on for every business.

<!-- TV1 -->
## A person is always one tap away

A visitor who asks for a person ("talk to a person", "agent", "insaan se baat karni hai", "call me") gets your contact options on the first ask. This holds in every state: when your balance has run out, while your website is still being learned, after off-topic questions, at the monthly question limit, under fair-use limits and when the engine is busy. Asking for a person is free: it is not counted as a question and costs nothing.

- **The button.** Every chat shows a **Talk to a person** button in its header. The platform operator decides whether an owner may hide it.
- **After a miss.** When the assistant has no answer, it offers your contact options straight away. Under **Assistant, Talk to a person**, change **Offer a person after the assistant can't answer** (Never, or after 1 to 5 unanswered questions; questions it had to turn down count too). The default is 1. It offers once by itself, and a visitor can always ask again.
- **No loops.** If the assistant would give the same reply twice in a row, the next reply is your contact card.
- **Nothing to offer.** If your business card has no phone, WhatsApp, e-mail or website and no call back, the visitor is told to contact you directly. Add at least one way to reach you.
- **Outside opening hours** the visitor sees when you open again and can ask for a call back.

## Nobody has to repeat themselves

When a visitor moves from the assistant to a person, the person already knows what was asked.

- **A reference.** The chat gets a short reference such as `SD-4F7K` the first time a person is offered, or when the visitor leaves their details. It stays the same for the whole chat. Owners find the chat by typing it into **Find a reference** on the Handoffs tab of **Leads**.
- **WhatsApp.** The message opens with "Ref SD-4F7K" and the visitor's last few questions already typed. The visitor can read and change it before sending.
- **Calls.** The card says "Your reference: SD-4F7K, quote it if you call", so whoever picks up can find the chat.
- **Your team's e-mail.** A request for a person or a call back reaches your team with a short summary, the reference and a link to the chat. A lead e-mail carries the same reference, and so does the data sent to your own systems and the exported list of leads.
- **Privacy.** Phone numbers and e-mail addresses are hidden in the summary unless the visitor left their details with you. The consent notice visitors read before sharing their details now says that a short summary and a reference go to your team with them. If you wrote your own wording, it stays as you wrote it; under **Assistant, Leads** you are offered the recommended wording to use in its place.

## No surprises

Your plan page tells you what is coming before it arrives, and nothing about the price can change under you.

- **Renewal.** The **Next renewal** card gives the day and the exact amount, and you are e-mailed a few days before (7 by default) with that same amount. If a new price starts with that renewal, both the card and the e-mail say so.
- **Where the month is heading.** "About ₹3,000 this month at this pace" is worked out from what usage has cost so far, plus your plan fee if it renews before the month ends. It is a guide, not a promise.
- **Price changes come with notice.** When your provider raises a price, every owner on the plan is e-mailed at least 30 days ahead (the provider can lengthen this). The change is listed under **Price changes coming**, and it applies from your own next renewal after the start date, never in the period you were told in. A lower price starts at once. A raise just for your business waits in the same way, unless you asked for it.
- **A spending cap you control.** Owners set a **Monthly spending cap** in rupees on the plan page. It counts what usage charges, not your plan fee. You are told at 80% and at the cap. At the cap your assistant switches to a slower model if your provider has one, or pauses and shows your contact details, until the month ends. A visitor who asks for a person is never refused, whatever the cap says.
- **Support that answers.** **Get help** opens COB first. If you are still stuck, **Contact** your provider writes a support request with the page you were on and your plan attached. You see its status and the reply on the same page, and by e-mail. Your provider may show how fast they usually reply.

## What the provider controls
<!-- audience: admin -->
Under Sign-up & branding the provider sets the renewal reminder days, the notice for a price rise (7 to 90 days), the highest spending cap an owner may choose, whether support requests are on and the reply time shown. A price change that makes any business pay more is scheduled in Billing, with a start date at least the notice period away, and the owners are e-mailed; a cancelled change is e-mailed too. The Support inbox shows every request with the workspace information that came with it, and the Price list page makes a white-label list of plans and add-ons to print or share by a link that can be turned off.

## Value report

Every month your assistant's work is added up for you: conversations, questions answered without staff, answers after hours, leads, requests to talk to a person, bookings, first-reply time, what people asked most and the questions it could not answer. On top sits an **estimated value** in rupees, always shown with its formula and always "based on your settings": qualified leads times what a lead is worth to you, requests to talk to a person times what one is worth, and the minutes saved times what an hour of staff time costs. It is an estimate for your own planning, never a promise.

- **Where.** A **Value this month** card on Home, the full report under **See the full report**, and an e-mail on the 1st of every month with last month's report. A month with no conversations is not e-mailed.
- **Your values.** Owners set the four numbers (a lead, a request to talk to a person, an hour of staff time, and the minutes saved per answer) under **Change values**, or start from the suggested values for their kind of business. Until then the suggestions are used and the report says so.
- **Frozen months.** A finished month never changes, even if you change your values later.
- **Print.** **Print or save as PDF** opens a page ready to print.

## Your clients' reports and the cc option
<!-- audience: admin -->
The provider sees each business's report on the **Value** tab of its detail page, exactly as the owner does. Under the engine settings the provider can switch the value report off for everyone (no e-mail and no Home card; owners keep their data) or add the support address in copy to every monthly e-mail.

## Honest about being an AI, and about how fresh answers are

Visitors are never left guessing who they are talking to, or how old an answer is.

- **An AI badge and a first line.** Every chat header shows an **AI assistant** badge, and the first message of every chat says it is an AI, in the visitor's language. An owner can reword the line if the provider allows it, as long as it still says AI. By default the provider requires both, so an owner cannot switch them off. On WhatsApp there is no header, so the first reply of every chat starts with the line as a message of its own, and the assistant is told to say it when asked who it is even if the owner switched their own line off.
- **When was this updated?** Under an answer, the source chips say when that source last changed ("Updated 3 Oct"), and a visitor can ask. The date is the day the text last changed, so a page read again with no change keeps its date. **Knowledge** shows a **Changed** column for each page of your website and when it was last read.
- **Brochures and price lists.** The first-run steps now ask for them as step 2, and Home suggests adding them while your assistant has no files and has read only a few pages. Home also warns when visitors who ask for a person have no way to reach you.
- **Your leads, always yours.** **Export CSV** on the Leads page works on every plan, for owners, with the reference of each chat in its own column.

## What the provider controls
<!-- audience: admin -->
Under Sign-up & branding, **Telling visitors it is an AI** has two switches. **Always show the AI badge and first line** is on by default; turn it off only if your businesses must be free to hide it. **Let businesses word the first line** controls whether an owner's own wording is used (it must still say AI) or every chat uses the standard line.
