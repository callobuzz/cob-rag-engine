# Changing your plan

Owners can move their workspace to another plan from **Plan & billing**, under **Change plan**, when the provider lets businesses choose their own plan. Members do not see it.

Each plan shows its fee with the total including GST next to it, the credit it includes every period, its limits and whether your own AI key is included. Before anything is charged, the confirmation says exactly what will happen.

## Moving up

The new plan starts today. You pay only the difference between the two plans for the days left until your next renewal, taken from your balance, and the extra included credit for those days is added. From your next renewal you pay the new plan's full fee.

If your balance cannot cover the difference, **Continue to payment** takes you to the pay page. Without online payment, it asks your provider for a top-up instead, with the amount filled in. The plan starts as soon as the money arrives. A move waiting for its payment lapses after 7 days, or at your renewal if that comes first, and nothing is charged.

## Moving down

A cheaper plan starts at your next renewal, never in the middle of a period, and nothing is refunded for the days left. If you use more than the new plan allows, nothing is deleted: you just cannot add more until you are within its limits.

## Calling a move off

**Keep my current plan** calls off a move that is waiting for its date or its payment. Nothing is charged.

## When you cannot change

- **A change your provider set up is waiting** (`plan_change_pending_from_provider`): your provider scheduled a change to your plan; ask them.
- **This plan has a fee, and payments are not set up here** (`plan_change_needs_billing`): ask your provider to move you.
- **Your plan is renewing right now** (`plan_change_renewal_due`): try again in an hour.
- **This plan's price is not set yet** (`plan_not_ready`): ask your provider.

## Choosing a plan when you sign up

A **Start with Pro** (or **Start free**) button on your provider's pricing page brings the plan to the sign-up form. A plan with no fee starts at once. A plan with a fee starts once you pay for it from Plan & billing after you sign up; nothing is charged before that. If sign-up needs approval, the plan applies when your workspace is approved.
