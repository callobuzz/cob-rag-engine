The Price list turns your plans and add-ons into one clean page with your own name, logo and colour. You can print it, save it as a PDF, or send a link to someone who is thinking of signing up. It is for the platform operator.

## What you see

- **Plans on the list**: every active plan that is for sale. Switch a plan on to show it, and use the arrows to put the plans in order. With none switched on, every active plan that has a price is shown, cheapest first. The Internal plan is never listed.
- **Show add-ons**: adds the add-ons you sell, such as extra questions or credit packs, with what each gives and its price.
- **Note**: a few lines under the list, for example "Prices exclude GST." It is plain text.
- **Preview**: the page exactly as a visitor will see it. Choose **Print or save as PDF** to open it in a new tab and use your browser's print dialog.
- **Public link**: create a link anyone can open. Making a new link stops the old one at once. **Remove the link** turns it off.

Each plan shows its fee for the period, the credit included, the price per question, what happens past the limits and the four limits in words. A price you have announced is shown as "from" its date, next to today's price. The page never shows your markup or what the AI costs you.

When you have a GST number, every fee and add-on price also shows its **total with GST** underneath, such as "₹1,178.82 a month incl. GST", so a prospect never meets GST as a surprise at checkout. If your prices already include GST, set **Prices include GST** in Billing → Tax and the list says so instead.

## Common tasks

1. **Send prices to a prospect.** Choose **Create a link**, copy it and send it. They see the page without signing in.
2. **Print for a meeting.** Choose **Print or save as PDF**.
3. **Change what is listed.** Switch plans on or off, reorder, then **Save**. The preview updates.
4. **Stop sharing.** Choose **Remove the link**, or **Make a new link** to replace it.

## Troubleshooting

- **A plan is missing**: it may be switched off, the Internal plan, or without a price when you have not picked plans yourself.
- **The old link still opens**: make a new link; the previous one stops straight away.
- **The note does not fit**: keep it under 500 characters.
- **The print tab is blank or blocked**: allow pop-ups for this page and try again.
