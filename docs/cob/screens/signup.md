Signing up creates a workspace for your business. One workspace per business; you can invite your team afterwards. What happens after you sign up depends on the mode the platform operator chose.

## What you see

- Fields for **Business name**, **Your name**, **Work email** and **Password**, with a strength meter and the requirements (10 to 128 characters, not your e-mail name, not a very common password).
- A note under the e-mail: "We'll send a 6-digit code to check it's yours."
- In approval mode a notice: new workspaces are reviewed by the team before they go live.
- When the operator has published both the Terms and the Privacy notice, a box: **I agree to the Terms and have read the Privacy notice**. Both words open the page in a new tab, and the form will not send until the box is ticked. If the pages change while you are reading, the box clears and you are asked again.
- **Your plan**, when you came from a **Start with Pro** (or **Start free**) button on the pricing page: the plan's name and its price with GST. A plan with a fee starts once you pay for it from Plan & billing after you sign up; nothing is charged before that. Without a plan in the link, or with one that cannot be chosen, you start on the default plan. In approval mode the plan applies when your workspace is approved.
- **Create account**, and **Already have an account? Log in**.
- When the operator has closed sign-up the page shows **Talk to us to get started** with a **Contact us** link instead of a form. When sign-up is unavailable it says so.

The three modes: **Open** (you verify your e-mail and start at once on the default plan), **Approval required** (you verify your e-mail, then wait while the operator activates you; you get an e-mail when it is ready) and **Closed** (no form; contact the operator, who adds you).

## Common tasks

1. **Sign up.** Fill the form, choose **Create account**, then enter the 6-digit code from your e-mail on the next screen.
2. **Wait for approval.** After verifying you see "We'll activate your workspace soon". You can close the page; an e-mail arrives when you are in.
3. **Come back later.** Log in with the same e-mail; if you are still pending the status page shows that.
4. **Get added by the operator.** In closed mode, contact them; they invite you with a link.

## Troubleshooting

- **`weak_password`**: choose a stronger password; the page lists what is missing.
- **`signup_closed`**: sign-up is closed. Use the contact link shown.
- **The code never arrives**: check spam, wait a minute and use **Resend code**. If the operator has not set up e-mail, ask them for the code.
- **"You already have an account"**: if that address is registered you get an e-mail instead of a code; log in or reset your password.
- **`rate_limited`**: too many attempts; wait the time shown.
- **"Sign-up isn't available right now"**: new workspaces cannot be created at the moment.
