This is the page where you pay. You reach it from **Plan & billing**: choose **Add money**, or **Pay plan fee**. Only owners can pay. Members see **Ask an owner to pay**.

## What you see

- **What you are paying for** in one line, with the GST worked out, for example "₹1,000.00 for your balance + ₹180.00 GST = ₹1,180.00 to pay". The balance receives the amount before GST (₹1,000.00); the GST is paid to the government and is on your tax invoice.
- **Pay** opens the payment window, where you pay with UPI, a card or net banking. You never type card details on this page.
- A **TEST MODE** note when your provider is still trying payments out: no real money moves.
- Links to the **Terms** and the **Refund policy**, when your provider has published them.
- Afterwards, a message such as "₹1,000.00 added. Your balance is now ₹2,240.50", or "We're confirming your payment…" while the bank finishes. You can leave the page: your balance updates by itself.

## Your tax details

Before the first payment we need the name and address that go on your tax invoice. Choose **Tax details** on Plan & billing: your business name, your address, your state and, if you have one, your GST number. Your GST number is optional. If you give it, your invoices show it so you can claim the GST back, and its first two digits decide your state. Write names and addresses in English letters.

## Common tasks

1. **Add money.** On Plan & billing choose **Add money**, pick or type an amount (the smallest top-up is ₹500), check the total, choose **Continue to payment**, then **Pay**.
2. **Pay your plan fee.** Choose **Pay plan fee** on Plan & billing. The amount is your plan's monthly fee plus GST.
3. **Change your tax details.** Choose **Tax details** on Plan & billing and save.
4. **See what you paid.** The **Payments** card on Plan & billing lists each payment with its date, amount, GST and a short reference you can quote to your provider.

## Troubleshooting

- **"We're confirming your payment…" for a long time**: the bank has not answered yet. If money left your account it will show up here shortly, and nothing is lost. If it does not appear within an hour, tell your provider the reference from your bank.
- **"Please add your billing details first"**: save your tax details, then try again.
- **"The payment window was closed"**: nothing was charged. Choose **Pay** to try again.
- **"Online payment is not switched on yet"**: your provider has not set up online payments. Use **Request top-up** on Plan & billing instead.
- **A refund**: ask your provider. Refunds come out of your unused balance, go back to the way you paid, and take 5 to 7 working days to reach your bank.
