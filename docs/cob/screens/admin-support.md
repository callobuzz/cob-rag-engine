The Support inbox is for the platform operator. Every business on your product can write to you from its dashboard, and the request lands here. You reply here; the business is e-mailed and sees your answer on its own Get help page.

## What you see

- **Numbers** at the top: how many requests are **waiting for you**, the **median first reply** time over the last 30 days, and the share **replied within your promise** when you have set one.
- **The list**: requests with the latest activity first, each with its number (#1042), the business name, the subject, a status and when it last moved. Filter by status, or search by number, subject or business name. A badge on **Support** in the menu shows how many are waiting.
- **The thread**: everything written, with who wrote it and when. Above it is **Sent with the request**: the plan, the state of the workspace, whether answers are paused and why, how many knowledge bases it has, the page the person was on, the product version and the last error codes the dashboard saw. It never includes a key, a balance or anything a visitor typed.
- **Reply**: write the answer and choose what the request becomes afterwards: **Answered**, **Waiting for the business** if you asked something, or **Closed**.

The first reply fixes the request's first-response time. A later reply never changes it. When a business follows up on a closed request, it opens again and you are e-mailed.

## Common tasks

1. **Answer a request.** Open it, write the reply, pick the status and **Send reply**. The business and the workspace owners get an e-mail.
2. **Change a status without replying.** Use the status menu beside the subject.
3. **Promise a reply time.** In Sign-up & branding, under notices and support, set the hours. Businesses then see "Usually replies within N hours".
4. **Turn requests off.** The same card has a switch. With it off, businesses see your contact details instead of a form.
5. **Choose where the e-mails go.** They go to the support address in your branding, or to the super admin when none is set.

## Troubleshooting

- **No e-mails arrive**: check the support address in Sign-up & branding and the e-mail setup. The requests are still here.
- **A business says it cannot send**: each workspace can send ten new requests a day.
- **The median shows a dash**: nothing has been answered in the last 30 days.
