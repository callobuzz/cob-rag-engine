The value report shows, in plain numbers, what your assistant did for your business, and then puts an **estimated** value in rupees on it. The estimate always comes with its formula and is based on the values **you** set, so treat it as a rough guide for your own planning. It is not a promise of any result. You open it from the **Value this month** card on Home, from **See the full report**, or from the e-mail that arrives on the 1st of every month.

## What you see

- **Estimated value**, in big numbers, with "Estimated value, based on your settings". The current month says **so far**. A finished month is frozen: it never changes afterwards, even if you later change your values.
- **How this is worked out**: three lines, one for each part of the estimate. For example "9 qualified leads × ₹1,500 = ₹13,500", "23 requests to talk to a person × ₹800 = ₹18,400" and "412 questions answered × 3 min saved = 20 h 36 min × ₹250/h = ₹5,150". Add them up and you have the total.
- **The numbers**: conversations, questions answered without staff, answers given after hours, qualified and partial leads, requests to talk to a person, bookings requested and your **First reply** time (the middle value across conversations).
- **What people asked most** and **Questions it couldn't answer**. Next to each unanswered question, **Add an answer** opens the Knowledge page with that question ready to answer, so next month's numbers get better.
- **Month**: choose this month or a finished one. A month before your workspace began, or one that has not happened yet, is not available.
- **Print or save as PDF** opens a printable page in a new tab; use your browser's print dialog and choose "Save as PDF".

Only what your visitors wrote is counted: the questions you ask yourself in the Playground or the first-run Try step never are. A **qualified lead** is a person who left contact details and met your lead rules. If you have not written any lead rules, every lead counts and the report says **leads** instead of qualified leads. Spam is never counted. A **Call me back** form is one request, not two: it is counted as the lead, or as a request to talk to a person when it does not count as a lead. **Answered without staff** means the assistant answered from your own content. **After hours** counts answers given while your business hours said you were closed; it needs hours on your business details, and counting started the day this feature began, so earlier months show **After-hours answers are counted from** that date.

## Changing your values

Owners choose **Change values**. Every number is in rupees:

- **What a lead is worth**, for example ₹1,500 for a patient who asks for an appointment.
- **What a request to talk to a person is worth**, for example ₹800.
- **What an hour of staff time costs**, for example ₹250 an hour.
- **Minutes saved per answered question**, between 0 and 120. The usual starting point is 3.

**Use suggested values for clinics** (or shops, coaching classes, restaurants and so on) fills in sensible starting numbers for your kind of business; you can change every one. Until you save your own values the report says it is using suggested ones. The switch **E-mail me the report on the 1st of every month** turns the monthly e-mail off or on. Members of your team can read the report but cannot change the values.

## Common tasks

1. **Check this month.** Open Home and read **Value this month**, then **See the full report**.
2. **Make the estimate yours.** Choose **Change values**, enter what a lead and an hour of staff time are really worth to you, and **Save values**.
3. **Look at an earlier month.** Pick it under **Month**.
4. **Keep a copy.** Choose **Print or save as PDF**.
5. **Teach the assistant.** Under **Questions it couldn't answer**, choose **Add an answer**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The total is ₹0**: your values may be zero, or there were no leads, requests to talk to a person or answered questions yet. The counts still show what happened.
- **After-hours answers are zero for an earlier month**: counting started later than that month, so those answers are not included. Add your opening hours to your business details so they are counted from now on.
- **"That month has not happened yet"** or "did not exist": pick a month from the list.
- **No e-mail on the 1st**: a month with no conversations is not e-mailed, and you or your provider may have switched the e-mail off. The report is always here.

## Your clients' reports and the cc option
<!-- audience: admin -->
Each business's report is also on its **Value** tab in the customer detail, in the same words, with the same printable page; it is read only. Under **Branding and engine settings**, the provider can switch the value report off for everyone (no e-mail and no Home card, though owners keep their data), or add itself in copy to every monthly e-mail, which goes to the support address.
