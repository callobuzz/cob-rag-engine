Your assistant answers only from what is here. It learns from your website, from articles and answers you write, and from files you upload. Owners can delete things.

New here? The [first-run steps](/docs/screens/welcome) set up your website, your assistant and its code in one go.

## What you see

At the top, "What your assistant knows" and, only if you have more than one knowledge base, a picker. Below it, two tabs:

- **From your website**: your assistant reads your own website by itself. For each website you see how many pages it learned of how many it found, when it was last read and its status (**Up to date**, **Learning your pages**, **Paused** or **Couldn't read this website**). Below that, **Pages** lists every page and what happened to it: **Learned**, **Waiting**, **Skipped** (with the reason, such as "Login or private page", "Same as another page" or "Beyond your plan's page limit"), **Excluded by you**, **Couldn't read** or **Removed from your site**. **Learning settings** switches reading on or off and lists pages to leave out, such as /blog/*. **How often should we check your website?** sets the schedule (default: every day). **Update instantly when you publish** gives your website a link to tell us about a new page. Your assistant reads only the websites you listed as allowed sites of your website widget, and only public pages.
- **Articles & files**: everything you wrote or uploaded. Each row shows the title, whether it is an **Article**, a **Question & answer** or a **File**, and when it was last updated. Articles and questions and answers have an **Edit** button; every row has **Delete** for owners.

Under the title, **Your website was last read 2 hours ago** links to **Read everything again**.

**Pages** also shows **Changed**, the day each page's text last changed. That date appears under answers as "Updated 3 Oct".

## Common tasks

1. **Write an article.** Open **Articles & files**, choose **Write an article**, add a title and your text, then **Save and teach my assistant**. The **Heading**, **Bullet** and **Bold** buttons help you format it, and the preview shows how it will read. Your assistant learns it in a moment.
2. **Add a question and its answer.** Choose **Add a question & answer**. Write the question the way people ask it, such as "Do you open on Sunday?", and the answer you want them to get, such as "Yes, 10 am to 2 pm."
3. **Upload files.** Choose **Upload files** and drop in a price list, menu, brochure or any document. Progress shows under the list.
4. **Change something.** Choose **Edit** next to an article or an answer, change the text and save. Your assistant learns the new version; the old one stays in the document's history.
5. **Teach it your website.** Add your website as an allowed site of your website widget, then open **From your website** and choose **Learn this website**. It reads the most important pages first, such as your home page, services and contact page.
6. **Read a page again, or leave one out.** In the page list, use **Read again** after you change a page, or **Leave out** for a page your assistant should not use. **Read everything again** only learns what changed.
7. **Choose how often your website is checked.** Pick a schedule under **How often should we check your website?** A page is only read again when it has changed, so checking often costs nothing extra. **Check pages that change often more often** checks busy pages sooner and quiet ones later.
8. **Update instantly when you publish.** Choose **Create update link** (owners only), copy it at once (it is shown only now) and follow the steps for WordPress or any website. Publishing a page then makes your assistant read it within a minute. **Create a new link** stops the old one; **Remove link** switches it off.
9. **Bring back a removed page.** **Removed from your site** means your website said twice, an hour apart, that the page is gone. Its history stays: restore it from the document's history in Documents. If the page returns, it is learned again.
10. **Make your assistant forget something.** Choose **Delete** next to it (owners only) and confirm.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Your plan allows N documents"**: each article, answer and file counts as one document. Delete something you no longer need, or ask about a bigger plan on Plan & billing.
- **My new article is not in the list yet**: it shows as "Your assistant is learning…" and appears in a few seconds.
- **Delete is greyed out**: only owners can delete. Ask an owner in your team.
- **"Learning from your website isn't included in your plan"**: your provider decides this per plan. Ask them to switch it on.
- **A page I changed still shows the old text**: it is read again on your schedule. Choose **Read again** next to it to read it now.
- **A page I deleted is still there**: it leaves your answers only after your website says it is gone twice, an hour apart, so a short outage does no harm.
- **"Beyond your plan's page limit"**: your plan reads only a number of pages from your website, the most important first. Ask about a bigger plan, or leave out pages you do not need.
