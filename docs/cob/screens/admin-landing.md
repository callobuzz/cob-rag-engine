The public website is a set of pages at your own address: a home page, Pricing, Features, About and Contact, plus your legal pages and links to sign up and sign in. The home page plays a short sample conversation, so people see what they would get. It is for the platform operator only, and stays off until you publish it and switch it on.

## What you see

- **The top bar**: a switch to show the website to visitors, its address, when it was last published and by whom, and the buttons **Save draft**, **Preview** and **Publish**. When the switch cannot be turned on, the bar says why: publish once, or set the address.
- **Address**: where the website lives, like `https://www.sharmadental.in`, with help on the DNS record.
- **Ask it yourself**: a button that lets a visitor try your own assistant, with a sentence saying whether it is connected.
- **Home page**: the sections of the home page. Each can be switched on or off and moved up or down, or dragged. The first and the last stay where they are. **Start from an example** fills in a sample for a kind of business. **Write it from my knowledge base** writes the sample conversation.
- **Look**: your brand colour with a readability report for light and dark, the six font pairings, your logo, a poster picture and a video.
- **Pricing page**: which plans become cards, the credit explanation, the price calculator and the questions.
- **Other pages**: Features, About and Contact.
- **Menu & footer**: the menu links, the footer columns, a bar at the top and the texts Google and chat apps show.
- **Languages**: switch Hindi and Hinglish on. This tab lists every text still missing, each with a link to the field.
- **Versions**: every published version, newest first, with a **Go back to this** button.

Each text shows how many characters you have used and stops at the limit. Nothing is saved until **Save draft**, and visitors see nothing new until you publish.

## Common tasks

1. **Switch the website on.** Fill in what you want, press **Publish**, set the address, then turn on **Show the website to visitors**. The switch stays off until something is published and an address is set.
2. **Set the address.** Type the full address starting with `https://` in **Address** and save.
3. **Start from an example.** Open **Home page**, press **Start from an example**, pick the kind of business, tick the parts it may replace and apply. It only changes your draft until you save.
4. **Offer Hindi and Hinglish.** Open **Languages** and switch them on. Write each missing text, using the **Write it** links, or switch the language off again before publishing.
5. **Change the brand colour.** Open **Look** and pick a colour. The report shows how readable every text is, and says where the colour was darkened or lightened.
6. **Add a video.** In **Look**, upload a poster picture first, then an MP4 or WebM video, and press **Show the video on the home page**.
7. **Write the sample conversation from your content.** Press **Write it from my knowledge base**, choose one of your own knowledge bases, read the result and press **Use this conversation** or **Discard**.
8. **Connect Ask it yourself.** Choose one of your own knowledge bases and press **Connect**. It reuses a key that already allows your website, or makes one named "Public website", and never changes your other keys. Then switch on **Show the button** and save.
9. **Publish and go back.** Press **Publish**, add a note, and confirm. To go back, open **Versions**, press **Go back to this** and publish again.

## Good to know

- Nothing costs AI money when a visitor opens a page. The sample conversation is written text that plays on the page, and the real assistant starts only when a visitor presses the Ask it yourself button.
- **Write it from my knowledge base** uses one answer from your own AI model. The result is a draft for you to review and is never published by itself.
- Messages sent from the contact form arrive in Support, marked "From your website".
- While the website is off, the main address opens this dashboard. Someone who is already signed in and opens the main address goes straight to the dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

- **The switch is off and cannot be turned on**: publish at least once and set the address.
- **The Ask it yourself button is not on the website**: it is hidden until it is switched on and connected. Check the sentence under **Ask it yourself**: a switched-off key, a key that does not allow your address, or a deleted knowledge base each need **Connect** again.
- **Publish says texts are missing in a language**: fill in the fields it lists (each is a link), or switch that language off under **Languages**.
- **Someone saved this website in another tab**: two people edited at once. Your changes stay on screen. Reload to see theirs and make your changes again.
- **A file cannot be deleted**: the website uses it. Remove it from the page first.
