Get help is where you write to the company that runs this product for you. Try COB first: it knows how the product works and can often answer straight away. If you are still stuck, send a support request here. You can reach this page from the **Get help** button at the top, or from **Contact** at the bottom of COB's panel.

## What you see

- **Your requests**: every request your workspace has sent, newest activity first, each with a reference number such as #1042, its subject and a status. The list can be filtered by status.
- **Statuses**: **Waiting** means your provider has not replied yet. **Answered** means they replied. **Needs your reply** means they asked you something. **Closed** means it is done.
- **The conversation**: open a request to read everything that was written, in order, with times. Your provider's replies are marked with their name.
- **A promise**: when your provider has set one, the page says "Usually replies within 4 hours". It is a target, not a guarantee.
- **New request**: a short subject and what happened. We also send the page you were on, your plan and the last few error codes you saw, so you do not have to copy them out. Nothing from your visitors is sent.

Everyone in the workspace, owners and members, can write a request and follow up. E-mails go to the person who wrote it and to the workspace owners when your provider replies.

## Common tasks

1. **Ask for help.** Choose **New request**, write a clear subject such as "WhatsApp button not showing" and describe what you did and what you saw, then **Send request**.
2. **Read a reply.** Open the request. You also get an e-mail with the reply.
3. **Answer a question from your provider.** Write in the box under the conversation and **Send**.
4. **Close a request that is done.** Choose **Mark as done**. Writing again opens it once more.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Support requests are off"**: your provider has switched this form off. Use the contact details they gave you, shown on the page.
- **You cannot send another request today**: each workspace can send up to ten new requests a day. Add to a request that is already open, or try again tomorrow.
- **No reply yet**: check the promised time at the top of the page. Replies also come by e-mail, so look in your spam folder.
- **Text looks odd**: messages are shown as plain text, so tags and links are not formatted.
