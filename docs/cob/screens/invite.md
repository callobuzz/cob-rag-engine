An owner or the platform operator invites you by e-mail. The link opens this page, which shows who invited you and to which workspace.

## What you see

- "Join Shree Labs", who invited you and the role: Owner or Member.
- If you have no account yet: **Your name** and a password field, and **Accept and join**.
- If you already have an account with that address: **Log in to accept**.
- A line: "This invitation works once and expires on 9 Oct". Invitations last 7 days and each link can be used once.
- If the link cannot be used, "This invitation can't be used" and **Ask for a new invitation**.

Invitations work even when the workspace's operator runs closed sign-up, and even when e-mail is not set up (the sender copies the link and sends it to you themselves).

## Common tasks

1. **Join as a new person.** Enter your name and a password and choose **Accept and join**. You land in the workspace.
2. **Join with an existing account.** Choose **Log in to accept**, log in, and the invitation is applied.
3. **Join a second workspace.** Log in with the same e-mail; switch workspaces from the account menu.
4. **Ask for a fresh link.** Contact whoever invited you; they can resend it from the Team screen.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This invitation link has expired or was already used"** (`invitation_invalid`): ask for a new one.
- **"This invitation is for ... Log out and log in as that person"** (`invitation_email_mismatch`): the link only works for the invited address.
- **"You already have an account. Log in to accept"** (`account_exists`).
- **`already_member`**: you are already in that workspace.
- **"This invitation link is incomplete"**: open the whole link from the e-mail.
- **`weak_password`**: choose a stronger password.
