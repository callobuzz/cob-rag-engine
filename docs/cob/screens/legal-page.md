The legal pages are the public pages the service publishes about itself: the Terms of Service, the Privacy Notice, the Refund and Cancellation Policy, the Shipping and Delivery Policy, the Acceptable Use Policy, the Data Processing Addendum, the list of sub-processors and the Grievance Officer. Each one is a plain web page that opens without signing in.

## What you see

- The name of the service and the page title, then "Version" and "Last updated" so you know which text you are reading.
- The text of the page, in short headed sections.
- At the bottom, links to the other published pages.

A new version is a new page of text; older versions are kept on record. When you sign up while the Terms and the Privacy notice are both published, the sign-up form asks you to tick a box saying you agree to the Terms and have read the Privacy notice. The version you accepted is recorded against your account.

## Common tasks

1. **Read before you sign up.** On the sign-up page the words **Terms** and **Privacy notice** next to the box open the pages in a new tab.
2. **Find the pages later.** The links sit under the sign-in and sign-up pages, and at the bottom of every signed-in page.
3. **Complain or ask about your data.** Open the Grievance Officer page. It has a name, an e-mail address and a postal address, and promises an acknowledgement within 48 hours and an answer within 30 days.
4. **Ask for your data or ask for it to be erased.** The Privacy Notice explains how, and when you can expect an answer.

## Troubleshooting

- **The page says it is not published**: the service has not published that page yet. Ask the support contact shown on the sign-in page.
- **A link opens the wrong page**: use the address in the footer, which always points to the current version.
- **I accepted an older version**: nothing is lost. The record shows the version you saw, and a newer version applies from when the service announces it.
