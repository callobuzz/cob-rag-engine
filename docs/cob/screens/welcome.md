These four short steps take you from nothing to an assistant answering questions on your own website. The first time an owner signs in to an empty workspace, this page opens by itself. You can skip it, come back to it from **Home**, and it remembers which step you were on.

## What you see

**Step 1, your website.** You type your website the way you say it, such as `shreedental.in`. You then name your assistant (for example "Shree Dental Assistant"), choose its picture, pick what kind of business you run (Clinic, Shop, Coaching, Software, Real estate, Government office, Restaurant or Other), and can add a WhatsApp number and your opening hours. Choose **Continue** and everything is set up for you: your knowledge base, the assistant, the code for your website, and the reading of your website. Your website is read only once you have listed it as an allowed site, which this step does for you.

<!-- TV5 -->
**Step 2, brochures and price lists.** Optional. Add the files visitors ask about, such as a price list or menu, as PDF or pictures. A line shows how many files your assistant has read. Choose **Next**, or **Skip this step** and add files later in **Knowledge**.
<!-- /TV5 -->

**Step 3, add it to your website.** You see a box with your website code and a **Copy code** button. Choose where your website is built (**Any website**, **WordPress**, **Wix**, **Shopify**, **Squarespace** or **Google Sites**) and follow the short steps shown. **Send to my developer** writes an email or a WhatsApp message with the steps and the code, ready to send to whoever looks after your website. Below the code, a light shows **Waiting for your website…** and turns green with **Connected** the moment your assistant loads on your site.

**Step 4, try your assistant.** You chat with your assistant right here, with its own name and picture. Suggested questions come from your own pages, in English or Hinglish. A line shows **Learning your website: 23 pages read** and counts up while your assistant reads. Until it has read your pages, it tells visitors politely that it is still learning and shows how to reach your team.

Members of your team see a note that the owner is setting things up. Only owners can change anything here.

## Common tasks

1. **Start.** Type your website, check the name, and choose **Continue**. If something goes wrong, you see the reason in plain words and **Try again**. Nothing that already worked is lost or done twice.
2. **Add a picture.** Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP picture. A square one works best. We resize it for you. You can change it later on the **Assistant** page.
3. **Add the code to your website.** Copy the code, paste it just before the closing `</body>` of your pages, and publish your website. On WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Squarespace and Google Sites the steps are shown in the tab.
4. **My site uses www.** If you typed `shreedental.in` but visitors use `www.shreedental.in`, both are allowed for you. If the light shows that your assistant loaded on another address, choose **Allow** next to it and reload your website.
5. **Skip and come back.** Choose **Skip for now** at the top of the page to leave the steps, or **Skip this step** on the brochure step. On **Home**, the steps stay one click away: a card to carry on while they are unfinished, and **Open the setup steps** afterwards.
6. **Finish.** On step 4, choose **Go to my dashboard**. Home then shows your assistant, what it learned from your website and today's numbers.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Enter your website like shreedental.in"**: type only the address of your website. A full link with a page name works too, but we read the whole site either way.
- **"We can't reach that website from here"**: check the spelling and that your website is public. Addresses on a private network cannot be read.
- **The light never turns green.** Check that you pasted the code on every page you want the assistant on, that you published your website, and that you opened it once afterwards. If you use a website builder, the page you test must be the published one, not the editor. After 15 minutes we stop checking and show what to look at; you can finish later from Home.
- **Google Sites.** The chat shows inside the box you embedded. The address we detect is a Google address, so allow the one we show if the light asks.
- **Squarespace.** Code injection needs a paid Squarespace plan. On a free plan, use another way to add code, or ask your developer.
- **My picture was refused.** Use a PNG, JPG or WebP image at least 64 pixels wide and tall. SVG and GIF files are not accepted. Your assistant is already set up; add the picture from the **Assistant** page.
- **"Your plan allows N websites or widgets"**: your plan has a limit. Ask your provider about a bigger plan on Plan & billing.
- **Visitors see "I'm still learning".** That is expected for the first few minutes while your website is read. It stops by itself when the first pass is done.
