This is the page where you make the assistant your own. Visitors on your website meet it by the name you give it, such as "Shree Dental Assistant", with your picture, in your tone. Members can edit; only owners can publish.

## What you see

At the top, a bar that shows whether it is live, with **Publish**. Below it, four parts:

- **Name and business details**: the name visitors read, the picture, your business name, what you do and the details it may quote, such as your address, opening hours and WhatsApp number.
- **How it talks**: a tone such as friendly, formal or short and direct.
- **Languages**: the languages it replies in, including Hinglish.
- **When your assistant isn't sure**: where it sends people when it cannot help, such as your WhatsApp number, a call back or your contact page.

**All settings** opens the full settings for the knowledge base: topics and limits, guidance rules, lead capture, versions and a preview. If nothing has been set up yet, you first choose a starting point that fits your business, then change the details.

## A person is always one tap away

A visitor who asks for a person gets the way to reach your team at once, every time. They can type it in English ("talk to a person", "call me"), Hindi or Hinglish ("insaan se baat karni hai", "manager se baat karni hai"), or tap **Talk to a person** in the chat header. It works even when your balance is zero, when your website is still being learned, when the engine is busy and at the monthly question limit, and it never counts as a question or costs anything. The one exception is abuse: an abusive message still gets the abuse reply.

- **Outside opening hours**, the visitor sees when you open again and can leave a call back request.
- **If your business card has no phone, WhatsApp, e-mail or website**, the assistant says it cannot connect them and to contact you directly. Add at least one on **Name and business details** so that never happens.
- **Offer a person after the assistant can't answer** (Talk to a person, "A person is always one tap away"): the assistant offers your contact options after this many unanswered questions (ones it could not answer or had to turn down). It offers once by itself; a visitor can always ask again. The default is 1, so a visitor is never left at a dead end. Choose **Never** to stop it offering by itself; a visitor can still ask.
- **Show the Talk to a person button**: the header button stays on unless the platform operator lets owners hide it.
- **Repeating itself**: if the assistant gives the same reply twice in a row, the next reply is your contact card instead of a third copy.

## Telling visitors it is an AI

**Tell visitors it is an AI** is locked on while your provider requires it. Chats always show an **AI assistant** badge and a first line such as "I'm Asha, the AI assistant of Shree Dental Clinic." You can reword **How it says so**, but it must still say "AI". If your provider has not allowed rewording, you see the standard line instead.

## Common tasks

1. **Rename your assistant.** Open **Name and business details**, change the name, **Save**, then **Publish**.
2. **Change the tone.** Open **How it talks**, pick a tone, **Save**, then **Publish**.
3. **Add your WhatsApp number.** In **When your assistant isn't sure**, turn on WhatsApp and enter the number, for example +91 98220 12345. **Save** and **Publish**.
4. **Try it before publishing.** Use **Preview** in the bar. Nothing changes for visitors until you publish.
5. **Switch it off for a while.** Open **All settings** and use the on and off switch on the Overview section.

## Troubleshooting

- **Publish is greyed out**: you have changes you have not saved. Save the part you are editing first. Members cannot publish; ask an owner.
- **Visitors still see the old name**: your changes are saved as a draft until you publish them.
- **The picture does not show**: use a link that starts with https and opens in a browser by itself.
- **Someone else saved first**: the page offers **Load their version** so you do not overwrite their change.
