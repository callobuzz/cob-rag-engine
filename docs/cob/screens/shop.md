**Add-ons and services** is a card on **Plan & billing**. It lists what your provider sells on top of your plan, with the price before GST and the total with GST next to it, for example "₹1,999.00 + GST · ₹2,358.82 incl. GST". Everyone in the workspace can look; only **owners** can buy.

## What you can buy

- **Your own AI key.** Answer with your own AI provider key instead of using your balance. It renews every month until you cancel. Some plans (such as Pro) include it, and then the card says **Included in your plan**.
- **Services.** Work done for you: installation help, website design and deploy, priority support. A service is a **one-time** purchase unless it says it renews. Some include months of support, counted from the day the order is marked done.
- **Credit packs.** Money added to your balance, sometimes with a bonus. You pay for these online, since they cannot be paid from the balance they add to.

## What you see

- Each item: its name, what it gives, whether it **Renews every month** or is **One time**, and a **Buy** button.
- An item you already have: **Active until 1 Nov** and **Cancel renewal**. After you cancel it says **Won't renew** and stays active to the end of the period you paid for.
- **Your orders** (a second card, once you have bought a service): each order with its status (**Requested**, **In progress**, **Done**, **Cancelled**), your own note, a note from your provider, and the date your included support runs until. **Ask about this order** opens a support request about it.

## Common tasks

1. **Buy from your balance.** Choose **Buy**, then **Pay from balance**. For a service you can tell the team what you need and when. The price before GST leaves your balance; the GST was already paid when you added the money. If your balance is too low, add money first.
2. **Pay online.** Choose **Buy**, then **Pay online** (card, UPI or net banking). You are asked for your tax details the first time, and you get a GST invoice ([guide](/docs/screens/invoices)).
3. **Use your own AI key.** Buy the add-on (or move to a plan that includes it), then paste your key under **Model & keys** of a knowledge base. Until then answers use your provider's AI and your balance.
4. **Stop the monthly own-key add-on.** On **Plan & billing**, choose **Cancel renewal** next to it. It stays active until the end of the paid month; nothing is charged again and nothing is refunded for the days left.
5. **Follow a service order.** Open **Your orders**. When the order moves, you also get an e-mail.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Your plan already includes this" (`feature_already_included`)**: your plan or an add-on already gives you this, so there is nothing to buy.
- **"The balance is too low" (`insufficient_balance`)**: add money first, or choose **Pay online**.
- **The own-key add-on ended by itself**: when it was due to renew your balance could not cover it. You are e-mailed once, and your key stays saved but unused. Add money and buy it again. Your workspace is never paused because of this.
- **"Using your own AI key isn't part of your plan" (`byok_not_in_plan`)**: saving a key needs Pro or the add-on. A key saved earlier stays saved and can be removed.
- **I cannot see the card or the Buy button**: the card shows only when your provider has put something in the shop; members see it without the Buy button.
- **A cancelled order**: cancelling an order does not refund anything by itself. Ask your provider to refund the unused balance.
